## Monthly YouTube chapter film · 4–8 minutes

1. **Cold open (0:00–0:20):** one vivid question or moment, not a fabricated headline.
2. **Context (0:20–1:15):** where this sits in the timeline; show date and source-backed map.
3. **Main story (1:15–4:30):** explain the people, choices, and stakes with licensed imagery and clear narration.
4. **What we know (4:30–5:30):** separate documented fact from uncertainty and present-day reconstruction.
5. **Why it matters (5:30–6:30):** connect this milestone to the next chapter.
6. **Invite (final 15 sec):** point to the next release, source notes, and event page.

Provide captions, transcript, chapters, image credits, and source list with each upload. For the May 20–21 anniversary, plan a live or near-live program only after confirming rights, staffing, and a reliable schedule.

## Reels/Shorts cutdown recipe · 20–60 seconds

- One hook in the first three seconds.
- One central fact, with the date visible.
- One visual idea: map, object, drawing, or present-day location.
- One question or invitation.
- Captions throughout; readable end card; no misleading breaking-news framing.
